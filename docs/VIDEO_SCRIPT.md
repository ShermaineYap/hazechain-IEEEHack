# Demo video script (target 4:00, limit 3–5 min)

**Setup before recording:** open the live dashboard in Chrome at 1440×900, zoom 100%. Open a second tab on Sepolia Etherscan with the EvidenceRegistry address. Record the screen with OBS or Loom, microphone on, 1080p. Speak at an easy pace; the script is about 520 words.

| Time | Screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:25 | Dashboard hero: the satellite map full of fire pixels | "This is Central Kalimantan this week. Between the 17th and 24th of October, NASA satellites recorded fifty-eight thousand fire detections in this one box. Seventy-nine percent are on drained peat, which releases about eight hundred tonnes of CO₂ for every hectare that burns. This smoke is the haze over Malaysia and Singapore right now." |
| 0:25–0:50 | Stay on map; slowly zoom toward the south coast | "Satellites see every fire. The problem is what happens next. Attribution is contested: a fire inside your concession is easy to deny. And communities that keep their land from burning are rarely paid for it, because the funder has to trust somebody's report. I'm Shermaine, and this is HazeChain." |
| 0:50–1:30 | Click a red ignition marker inside a red concession; popup opens | "HazeChain's AI clusters the raw pixels into fire events and finds where each one started. Here, this fire started inside an oil-palm concession, and the model's confidence is above our point seven threshold. For every polygon a fire touches, it asks one question: did the fire start here, or spread in from outside? Only starts count. Fires already burning when our record began get half confidence, because we can't see where they really started." |
| 1:30–1:50 | Click a green (fire-free) concession; card shows 0 pixels | "And this concession, ninety-nine percent peat, had zero fire pixels all week. That's the land manager a climate fund should be paying." |
| 1:50–2:10 | Tick "Fire risk, next 7 days" | "A gradient-boosted model trained on seven years of fire seasons forecasts next week's risk. On the 2025 season, which it never saw, it scores an AUC of point eight three against point six eight for the naive baseline." |
| 2:10–2:55 | Scroll to "Check the evidence yourself". Choose 22 Oct, click "Rebuild the root", then "Ask the contract" | "Now the part a judge or a court cares about. Every day, every detection, fire event and attribution is hashed into one Merkle root, written to Ethereum once, and never again. Your browser just downloaded twenty-three thousand rows, rebuilt the root itself, and it matches the root on Sepolia. I ask the contract whether this attribution row is part of the record: proven." |
| 2:55–3:10 | Click "Tamper with this row", then "Ask the contract" | "Now I change the confidence, the way someone might after a fire. The contract rejects it. Nobody, including us, can quietly edit this evidence." |
| 3:10–3:45 | Scroll to "Pay-for-no-fire pledges"; point at each row; open one Etherscan tx | "This is where the money moves. A funder locks money against one polygon for a fire season. When it ends, the oracle reports ignitions, citing an anchored day of evidence. This village and this concession stayed fire-free: paid automatically. This concession had five confident ignitions: slashed to a fire-suppression pool. And this one had a borderline point seven one attribution, so the company disputed with patrol logs, and settlement is frozen until an arbiter rules. Every step is a real transaction on Sepolia." |
| 3:45–4:05 | Back to the map, wide view | "A fire-free village programme in Riau cut burnt area ninety percent for about thirty thousand dollars a village. Keep a thousand hectares of peat from burning and that's under four cents a tonne of CO₂. HazeChain makes that kind of payment trustworthy enough for governments, commodity buyers and climate funds, and the same pipeline works on every peatland on Earth. Ahead of COP31, this is transparent, results-based climate finance. Thank you." |
