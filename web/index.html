Check the evidence yourself

Every day's satellite detections, fire events and attributions are hashed into one Merkle root, and that root is written to Ethereum once and never again. Your browser downloads the raw rows, rebuilds the root, and asks the contract whether a single row belongs. No wallet, no trust in us.

Pay-for-no-fire pledges

A funder locks money against one polygon for a fire season. When the season ends the oracle reports attributed ignitions, citing a day of anchored evidence. After a dispute window anyone can settle: no confident ignition pays the community or company; a confident ignition sends the money to a fire-suppression pool. Fires that spread in from outside never count.

How the AI decides

Three models, each simple enough to audit. Every output carries the model version, and that version is anchored with the evidence.

Group pixels into fires

DBSCAN clusters detections that touch in space and time (1.5 km, with 24 hours counted like 1.5 km). The earliest overpass marks the ignition; the brightest pixel breaks ties. Fires already burning when the record starts are marked censored, and their confidence is halved because the true start is unknown.

Decide who it belongs to

For every polygon a fire touches: did it start there or spread in? Only a start counts. Confidence multiplies persistence (more pixels, more certain), distance from the boundary (a VIIRS pixel is 375 m wide), and how much of the fire stayed inside. The escrow ignores anything below 0.70 and always allows a dispute.
